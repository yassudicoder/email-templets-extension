                Privacy

                • Everything is stored in this browser. There's no account with us, and our servers never store your data.
                • Emails are sent through Google's Gmail API directly from this browser.
                • Email text goes to the AI only when you click Write with AI or for emails that match an auto-reply rule you created — through our relay (never stored) for the built-in AI, or straight to your own provider.
